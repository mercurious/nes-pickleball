# PICKLEBALL

## Instruction Booklet

*Thank you for selecting the Pickleball Game Pak for your Nintendo Entertainment System-compatible console.*

Please read this instruction booklet to ensure proper handling of your new game, and then save the booklet for
future reference.

### Object of the game / game description

Serve underhand, let it bounce, and work your way to the kitchen line! Pickleball is the paddle game from the
Pacific Northwest that the whole family plays: a plastic ball with holes, plywood paddles and a badminton-size
court. Play singles or doubles against the computer, team up with a friend, or sit back and watch the pros. Five
skill levels. The first side to 11 points, leading by 2, wins the game.

### Names of controller parts and operating instructions

| control | during play |
|---|---|
| Control Pad | Moves the player: up toward the net, down away from the net, left and right |
| A Button | Hard shot: a drive, a volley, or a smash when the ball is high. Also serves the ball |
| B Button | Soft shot: a dink from the kitchen line, a drop from the back. Hold Down and press B for a lob |
| START | Begins the game. During play, pauses the game (the pause tone sounds); press again to continue |
| SELECT | On the title screen, moves the asterisk to the next option |

Controller 1 is for 1 player games and for the first player of 2 player games; Controller 2 for the second player.

### How to play

On the title screen, move the asterisk with SELECT (or Up and Down) and change an option with Left, Right or A:

- **1 PLAYER SINGLES**: you against the computer.
- **1 PLAYER DOUBLES**: you and a computer partner against a computer team.
- **2 PLAYERS DOUBLES**: you and a friend as a team against a computer team.
- **2 PLAYERS SINGLES**: you against a friend. Player 2 plays the far court.
- **EXHIBITION**: two computer teams play; watch and learn.
- **LEVEL 1 to 5**: the computer's skill, from BEGINNER to CHAMPION. The computer's shirt shows its level.
- **GAME TO 11 / 15 / 21**: the points needed to win (always by 2).
- **ONE GAME / BEST OF 3**: a single game or a match.
- **LETS: REPLAY / PLAY**: a serve that touches the net and lands in is replayed (the traditional rule) or played.

Press START to begin. Leave the title screen alone for half a minute and a demonstration match begins.

### The court

The court nearest you is your court; the far court is your opponents'. The lines are part of the court: a ball
that lands on a line is in. The light green area on each side of the net is the **non-volley zone**, which
players call **the kitchen**. You may not hit the ball out of the air while standing in the kitchen or on its
line, and you may not step into it right after a volley. A ball that has bounced may be hit from anywhere.

### Serving

Before every serve the server calls the score. The umpire shows the call: in singles "4-2" (the server's
score first), in doubles "4-2-1" (the serving team's score, the receiving team's score, and whether this is the
team's first or second server). Your player automatically stands behind the base line, on the right when your
score is even and on the left when it is odd, and holds the ball out. **Press A as the ball drops** to hit it
underhand across the court: wait too long and the ball bounces (FAULT); hit it too early and it flies past the
base line. The serve must land diagonally opposite, past the kitchen line. There is only one serve.

### The two-bounce rule

The serve must bounce before the receiver hits it, and the return must bounce before the serving side hits
it. After that, anyone may volley (outside the kitchen). Hit a serve or a return out of the air and the umpire
calls TWO BOUNCE: fault.

### Scoring

Only the serving side scores. When the serving side loses a rally in singles, the serve passes to the
opponent: SIDE OUT. In doubles both partners serve before a side out, except at the start of the game, when the
first serving team has only one server (that is why the first call of every game is "0-0-2"). The umpire's calls:

| call | meaning |
|---|---|
| OUT! | the ball landed outside the court |
| NET | the ball did not clear the net |
| LET | a serve touched the net and landed in: serve again |
| SHORT! | a serve landed in the kitchen |
| WRONG COURT | a serve landed in the wrong service court |
| FAULT | the serve was not hit before it bounced |
| TWO BOUNCE | the serve or the return was hit out of the air |
| KITCHEN! | a volley from the non-volley zone, or momentum carried the player into it |
| POINT | the serving side won the rally |
| SIDE OUT / 2ND SERVER | the serving side lost the rally |
| GAME | the game is over |

### Shots

| shot | how |
|---|---|
| Drive | A, from the back of the court |
| Volley | A, out of the air (not from the kitchen!) |
| Smash | A, on a high ball |
| Dink | B, at the kitchen line: a soft shot that lands in the opponents' kitchen |
| Drop | B, from the back: the third shot that lets you come forward |
| Lob | Down + B: high over the opponents at the line |

The direction of a shot depends on **when** you swing: meet the ball early (while it is still in front of you)
and it goes one way; meet it late and it goes the other way. Hit it in the middle and it goes straight. A
straight shot from near a sideline goes out: aim for the middle.

### Pickleball terminology

- **Kitchen**: the non-volley zone, 7 feet on each side of the net.
- **Dink**: a soft shot from the kitchen line into the other kitchen.
- **Third shot drop**: the serving team's first shot after the return, dropped softly so the team can come to the line.
- **Side out**: the serve passes to the other side.
- **Two-bounce rule**: the serve and the return must bounce before being hit.
