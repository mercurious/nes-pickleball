# Findings, 2026-10-12: building v0.1.0

The build-out of `docs/DOSSIER_v0.1.0.md`, steps 1-8, in one session on etk-cloud (builds and tests) and the
laptop (cc65, the encoder). The sound is described in `docs/SOUND.md`. What waits on the maintainer is at the
end.

## What was found

1. **1 PLAYER DOUBLES**: the partner never took a ball (the team's decision ran only from slot 0, the human).
   Fixed: 0 returns before, 85 after (`test/doubles.lua`).
2. **v0.0.1's sound effects never ended.** `fx_step` never advanced, so each effect's first step held until the
   next: a 445 Hz tone from the menu to the serve, then 695 Hz, then a 130 Hz drone under every rally, over the
   voice. Heard in the first recording the new rig made; the original ROM records the same.
3. **The main loop takes more than a frame about 4 frames in 10** (v0.0.1: 60 % of frames get an update, now
   54 %), so the game's logic runs at about 33-36 updates a second, unevenly. The game was tuned that way, so it
   stays (faster would mean re-tuning every speed), but the sound engine ran from that loop: the music played at
   about half tempo and every envelope stretched. Pently runs in the NMI now.
4. **The 2A03 corrupts DMC bytes.** A DMC fetch that meets a CPU read of $4000-$401F gets an internal register
   ($4015-$4017) instead of the byte whenever the sample's address ends in $15-$17. Mesen emulates it. The
   controller reads hit it every frame and knocked the voice 8 levels at a time off its resting point. The
   controllers are now read in step with the OAM DMA; nothing reads $4015; the level is set back after each
   sample for the rare hit from Pently's indexed stores (their dummy reads).
5. **The ball was drawn 10-12 px below where it was**, and its shadow: the art sits at the bottom of the 8x16
   sprite. Part of why a hit didn't look like it met the paddle.
6. **NES Tennis**, register by register: tonal hits (a 50 % pulse near 1 kHz swept down by the hardware sweep,
   0.4 s), a constant crowd murmur on pulse 2, no noise, no samples. A real pock is shorter: 20-25 ms.
7. **Frame-number deadlines wrap**: compared as a signed byte they looked unreached again 128 frames later, and
   hits went silent. Each deadline has a flag now; `test/cues.lua` checks every hit's pock.
